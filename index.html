Privacy Policy for My Light

Effective date: October 11, 2026

My Light ("the App", "we", "our") is an Android app that turns your phone's flashlight on and off when you shake the device. This policy explains what information the App and its third-party service providers use, and how it is handled. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location, and the App has no account or sign-up. We do not store any personal information on our own servers.

The App does use two third-party services, Google AdMob (ads) and Firebase Analytics (usage statistics), which automatically collect certain technical information from your device. This is described in Section 3.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following locally on your phone so it can work properly:

  • Your settings, such as shake sensitivity and the vibration on/off choice
  • A counter of how many times the flashlight has been turned on

This data stays on your device. It is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

3. Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising)

The App shows ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and use:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (such as device model, operating system version, language, and screen size)
  • App interaction and ad performance data (such as ad views and clicks)
  • Diagnostic information related to ads

Firebase Analytics (usage statistics)

The App uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google LLC, to understand how the App is used and to improve it. Firebase Analytics may automatically collect:

  • App instance ID (a random identifier for your installation of the App)
  • Advertising ID, where available
  • Device information (such as device model, operating system version, language, and screen size)
  • App usage events (such as app opens, first open, sessions, and how features are used)
  • IP address and approximate location (country or region) derived from it

We use this information only in aggregated form to analyze usage and improve the App. We do not use it to identify you personally.

How this data is handled

The information above is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy policies. We do not receive your name or other directly identifying information from Google. For details, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services
  • Firebase privacy information

Your choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's Settings (usually under Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads and before using analytics. You can also stop all data collection by uninstalling the App.

The App does not use any other analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking services of its own.

4. Permissions the App uses

PermissionWhy it is needed
CameraTo access the phone's flashlight (torch) and turn it on and off. The App does not take photos or record video.
Motion sensor (accelerometer)To detect a shake gesture. Sensor data is processed in real time on your device and is not saved or sent anywhere.
InternetTo load ads (AdMob) and send usage statistics (Firebase Analytics).
Foreground serviceTo keep shake detection running in the background, shown to you as an ongoing notification.
NotificationsTo display the notification required by Android while the service is running (Android 13 and above).
VibrationTo give vibration feedback when the flashlight toggles. This is optional and can be turned off in the App.
Receive boot completedTo restart shake detection after your phone reboots.
Ignore battery optimizationsTo ask Android not to stop the background service. This is optional and only requested with your approval.
Schedule exact alarmsTo restart the background service if the system stops it. On Android 12 and above, this is granted by you in system settings.

You can revoke any permission at any time in your phone's Settings, but some features may stop working if you do.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent your information. The only third party that receives data through the App is Google, through AdMob and Firebase Analytics as described in Section 3. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App's advertising and analytics services are not intended to be used with data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

7. Data security

Your settings and flashlight counter are not transmitted anywhere. They are kept in the App's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandboxing. Data handled by Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics is protected under Google's own security practices.

8. Data retention and deletion

Data stored by the App on your device remains until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Analytics and advertising data held by Google is retained according to Google's own retention policies. Because we do not hold personal data on our own servers, there is nothing for us to delete directly. You can manage Google's advertising data through your Google Account's ad settings.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with a new effective date. If you keep using the App after a change, you accept the updated policy.
